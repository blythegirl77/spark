Founder note

Four years ago I left my job as head of product to make murder mystery games. When it was time to market them, I wrote one week of posts. Then I quit.

The posts weren’t wrong. They sounded like everyone else’s, so nobody noticed. Years later, reading 50 dating profiles, I saw the same thing: six men told me they were funny, and only one of them made me laugh.

What changed it for me was seeing marketing differently: not convincing people I’m the best, but telling them who I am and why I do this, so they can decide for themselves.

This challenge is for the week I quit.

Lara Kehler
